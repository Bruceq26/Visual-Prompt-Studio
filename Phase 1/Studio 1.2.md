Prompt: Create a Heavy Metal / Gothic album cover called “SKIBIDI TOILET: RISE OF THE BRUCES.” I want a huge dark warrior in the middle of an old gothic city, kind of like Batman, with huge stone buildings and towers around him. Make him look really powerful and rich, like he owns the whole place, and make his size and mass give him most of the emphasis. I want the background to have dark values and deep shadows, but have one really bright light behind him to make the contrast stand out so he could look big and badder. The buildings should get smaller as they go back and some should overlap each other so it gives the image more space and depth make the building look very detailed like the ones that the Rome's used to build that took the millions of years which make the architecture look very detailed. Add smoke, flying rocks, cracks, and lines around everything to give it some rhythm and make it feel like things are moving. Keep some black, gray, and silver colors so the cover has unity. Give the rocks, metal, and buildings different textures for more variety. Use asymmetrical balance so the giant warrior is the main thing you notice first, and make the whole thing look realistic, creepy, powerful, and 3-D. Make the whole cover look dark, creepy, powerful, and like a Heavy Metal album. Lastly, adding a throne behind him. oh can you also add like a mini version of himself in there like if he has a kid of his own.

Space: The area that there is in the artwork so it could be like building all bunched up together but it can also be like the amount of empty mess  that there is in the area. Unity: Is how the artwork is being demonstrated for example using similar colors and textures being mixed together such as it making a whole of artwork instead of it just being a artwork that just has different things that just doesn't make since and looks good. Balance: the amount of stuff that is used to be incorporated in the picture will demonstrate how everything is put together like size, color, and even shapes. Rhythm: Having the repetition such as shapes, lines, figures, and much more which can always be pointed out when there are many of them
