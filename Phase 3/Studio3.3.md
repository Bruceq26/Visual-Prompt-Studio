Assignment title: Faith and Equality

Prompt: Create a landing page for an art exhibition called “Faith and Equality.” I want to mix Medieval Sacred Art with social justice by showing a woman surrounded by golden halos, stained-glass windows like the ones that your able to find in churches, and can you also add pictures of people protesting for equal rights. Make sure to use Cubism to break up the images into different shapes so the old religious art and modern protest scenes blend together. This is a very important step due to the fact that the point of the assignment is to be able to bring two things together and make it look into one. Make the gold colors stand out against the dark background making it look more eye-catching. Use the curved lines of the cathedral and the diagonal lines of the protest signs to lead the viewer’s eyes toward the woman. Lastly, to make her face the main focal point has to be the main thing standing out. Add a description of the exhibition, the dates, the location, ticket prices, and smaller images that represent equality, hope, and community.

Chosen Genre Mashup: My genre mashup is Medieval Sacred Art and Social Justice. I’m mixing old religious art with modern ideas like equality, protests, and people fighting for their rights. I want to show how art from the past can connect to problems and issues we still see today.

Glossary: Contemporary Art: Art that people make nowadays. Cubism: Art that uses a lot of shapes and different angles. Directional Lines: Lines that make you look at a certain part of the picture. Value Contrast: Using light and dark colors to make stuff stand out. Focal Point: The first thing you notice when you look at the picture.
